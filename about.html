About TapirFind

TapirFind was created with the goal of helping people to easily find new dining places in Malaysia. We provide a simple way to discover restaurants, get recommendations, and make reservations.

How recommendations work

  • Dietary & Lifestyle

    If you choose Vegetarian or Vegan, we only show restaurants that serve those options. Vegan kitchens also count for vegetarians.

  • Halal & Religious Status

    Choosing Muslim-Friendly or Halal will only show restaurants with those options. Halal restaurants also count as Muslim-Friendly.

  • Cuisine

    Pick a cuisine; restaurants that serve it will be prioritized in the recommendations.

  • Budget

    We compare your budget with each restaurant's average cost per person.

  • Dining purpose

    Different occasions call for different dining experiences; we recommend restaurants accordingly.

Reservations and costs

  • Small deposit

    Each restaurant requires its own set deposit, which is shown on the listing and automatically filled in for you when you choose to reserve the restaurant.

  • Voucher or card

    Pay the deposit with a 12-digit voucher code or an online card payment.

  • Know the cost first

    The bill estimate adds a 10% service charge and 6% tax to your chosen dishes, according to common taxation numbers in Malaysia; these may or may not apply in the actual restaurant bill.
